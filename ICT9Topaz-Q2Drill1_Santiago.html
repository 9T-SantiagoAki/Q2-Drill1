Beginner's Guide to Badminton

Basic Rules

  • Hit the shuttle over the net.
  • Keep the shuttle inside the court.
  • A game is played to 21 points.
  • Win two out of three games.

Equipment

  • Badminton racket
  • Shuttlecock
  • Net and court
  • Sports shoes
